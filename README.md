# FORESIGHT – Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

FORESIGHT is a Power BI-based Sales and Inventory Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze business performance, inventory health, product performance, promotion effectiveness, and sales patterns.

The project uses data processed with Python and presents the final analysis through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Track units sold and profitability
- Identify inventory risks and excess inventory
- Analyze product-level inventory performance
- Evaluate the impact of promotions on sales and profitability
- Understand seasonal, weekday, and holiday sales patterns

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV datasets

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales & Inventory Analytics
Provides an overall business overview through:
- Revenue
- Units Sold
- Profit
- Inventory Value
- Excess Capital
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Monthly Units Sold
- Inventory Coverage
- Inventory Risk Distribution

### 2. Product Performance
Analyzes product and promotion-related performance using:
- Revenue by Promotion Status
- Profit by Promotion Status
- Units Sold by Promotion Status
- Average Profit Margin by Promotion Status
- Average Price by Promotion Status

### 3. Inventory Analysis
Focuses on inventory health and risk:
- Inventory Status Distribution
- Inventory Value by SKU
- Inventory Ratio by SKU
- Excess Capital by SKU
- Demand vs Inventory Coverage

### 4. Promotion Performance
Evaluates the effect of promotions on:
- Revenue
- Units Sold
- Average Profit Margin
- Average Price

### 5. Sales & Seasonality Analysis
Analyzes sales patterns across:
- Revenue by Season
- Units Sold by Season
- Weekday/Weekend Sales
- Holiday vs Non-Holiday Sales

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

- Products with low inventory coverage
- Products associated with inventory risk
- Excess inventory and tied-up capital
- Differences between promotional and non-promotional performance
- Seasonal sales patterns
- Weekday and holiday sales behavior
